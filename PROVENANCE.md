# Hand tracking: recovered materials

The nine files in `source/`, the June 2013 report and the original student-project figure (`assets/project-preview.jpg`)
are unchanged by this document update. Compilation, camera operation and
tracking accuracy remain unverified under the conditions described in the README.

## Draft slides

[Real time hand tracking](docs/real-time-hand-tracking-draft-2013.pptx) is an
unfinished 59-slide draft that Sa'ar Zehavi shared with Aaron Wetzler in April 2013 and supplied
again on 2026-11-13. The presentation is preserved byte-for-byte:

```text
SHA-256 09c75624ddddf7dc6ade4bf092eded1ad28549b6e7cee6b0d499eeb984927fb5
Bytes   658892
```

It describes recognition, seed selection, segmentation and geometric hand-position
estimation, using hand-drawn illustrations and a third-party cartoon (slide 37). It retains placeholder instructions
and a first-order approximation step. When originally sharing the draft, the author
said he had already removed that step and still needed to revise the illustrations.
The deck therefore does not document the final algorithm or establish measured
accuracy, real-time performance or compatibility with current systems. The June
2013 report is a later record; the recovered source is archived separately.

The new attachment contained slides only. It supplied no additional source,
PMD SDK components, recordings or executable build. The existing source and
historical video link remain unchanged.
